Our Story

“We are first-year IT students at IGDTUW who experienced a common problem during exams — despite studying a lot, we couldn’t clearly identify what we actually understood and what we didn’t.”

“There was too much theory, and everything felt overwhelming. We didn’t know our weak topics, what we missed, or what to revise.”

“That’s when we thought — what if there was a system that could test us instantly and tell us exactly what we don’t understand?”

“This idea led to the creation of ConceptLens.”

🎯

Our Goal

“To help students move from confusion to clarity by diagnosing their understanding and guiding them towards smarter revision.”
