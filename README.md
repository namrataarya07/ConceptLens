# ConceptLens – AI Learning Auditor

### Know What You Don’t Understand

---

## 🧠 Overview

ConceptLens is an AI-powered system that audits a student’s understanding instead of just providing content.

It evaluates answers, detects knowledge gaps, and tells students exactly what they don’t understand and what they should revise.

> Not a study tool. A system that diagnoses learning.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

During our mid-semester exams, we faced a frustrating problem.

We studied notes, attended lectures, and revised multiple times — but still had no clarity on what we actually understood.

Everything felt overwhelming, especially in theory-heavy subjects like EVS.

We couldn’t identify:

* what we knew well
* what we partially understood
* what we completely missed

### 💡 Realization

Studying more content does not guarantee understanding.

There is a gap between:

* consuming information
* and actually understanding it

### 🚀 Idea

What if a system could:

* test us instantly
* tell us what we missed
* and guide us on what to revise

This led to the creation of ConceptLens.

---

## 💡 Solution

ConceptLens is an **AI Learning Auditor** that:

* evaluates student answers
* detects missing concepts
* provides structured feedback
* guides revision
